Guild Rules

Uphold the MEOW Code of Honor • Zero Compromise Protocols

01

Zero Toxicity Protocol

Respect every member and opponent unconditionally. Abusive language, derogatory remarks, ego-clashing, or racism result in instant guild banishment without prior notice.

02

Mandatory Activity

Maintain daily presence in scrims and team training. Accumulating 3 consecutive days of unexcused inactivity triggers automated slot revocation. Inform an Elder if personal leave is required.

03

Discord Synchronisation

All members must remain active in the official MEOW Discord server. Match schedules, custom room keys, tactical debriefs, and community directives are coordinated strictly through voice and announcement channels.

04

Chain of Command

Respect tactical calls issued by Elders and In-Game Leaders (IGLs) during scrims and tournament rotations. Disciplinary disputes must be submitted via the Support station.
